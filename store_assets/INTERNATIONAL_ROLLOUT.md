# StreamBeat international rollout

## Launch order

1. Brazil: publish the `pt-BR` app and store localization, localized screenshots,
   feature graphic, and subscription text.
2. Spanish-speaking Latin America: keep `es-419` as the primary Spanish
   listing and use the same four-feature screenshot sequence.
3. English-speaking markets: use `en-US` for the United States, Canada, United
   Kingdom, Australia, India, the Philippines, and other English-language
   traffic.
4. Expand only after acquisition and retention data identify the next
   languages. Initial candidates are French, German, Indonesian, and Hindi.

## Store listing sequence

Use this order in every localized listing:

1. Home — worldwide discovery and recent listening.
2. Radio — country and genre filters.
3. Map — visual international discovery.
4. Settings — language, personalization, and continuous playback.

Every screenshot should receive its matching localized alt text from
`listing/screenshot-alt-text.txt`.

## First experiment

Run a localized Brazilian Portuguese experiment only after the listing has
enough traffic to produce a useful result.

- Target metric: unique user install clicks.
- Audience: use Play Console's recommended setting.
- Change one asset only: short description.
- Control: `Rádios do mundo ao vivo, mapa interativo, favoritos e playlists.`
- Variant: `Ouça rádios ao vivo do Brasil e do mundo em um mapa interativo.`
- Do not apply a winner until Play reports a conclusive result.

## Acquisition channels

- Organic ASO: localized title, descriptions, screenshots, and regular release
  notes in `pt-BR`, `es-419`, and `en-US`.
- Short-form video: 15–25 second demonstrations of finding a country on the
  map, saving a station, and sharing it. Reuse the same edit with localized
  captions for Reels, TikTok, Shorts, and Facebook.
- Creator outreach: focus on music discovery, language learning, expat, travel,
  and regional-culture creators. Offer a direct Play link; do not buy reviews.
- Community distribution: share useful station collections in relevant Reddit,
  Facebook, Discord, Telegram, and WhatsApp communities only where promotion is
  allowed.
- Paid acquisition: start with a small Brazil-only Google App campaign after
  organic conversion data exists. Route campaign traffic to a Portuguese custom
  store listing and stop ads that fail the retention target.

## Weekly scorecard

Track by country and language:

- Store listing visitors and unique install clicks.
- Store listing conversion rate.
- First successful station play.
- Day-1 and day-7 retention.
- Share-link creation and successful share-link opens.
- Favorite or playlist creation.
- Crash-free users and ANR rate.
- Rating, review volume, and recurring review themes.

The first product goal is not raw installs. It is qualified installs that reach
a first successful play and return within seven days.
