App name
StreamBeat: Live Radio

Short description
Live radio worldwide: explore by map, save favorites, and build playlists.

Full description
Explore the world through live radio with StreamBeat.

Discover stations from different countries, find music and shows by genre, and listen in a fast, visual experience.

With StreamBeat you can:
• Listen to internet radio stations from around the world.
• Explore stations by country, region, and genre.
• Browse an interactive map to discover new sounds.
• Save favorite stations and organize your own playlists.
• Review your playback history.
• Share stations with friends using direct links.
• Personalize the theme, accent color, and continuous playback.
• Use the app in English, Spanish, or Brazilian Portuguese.

Location is optional and is used only while you use the app to provide country-based suggestions and center the map. You can listen to radio without granting this permission.

StreamBeat may show one compact banner while no station is playing. An optional monthly subscription removes ads. Creating an account is optional and is used to access subscription benefits across compatible devices.

Station availability and audio quality depend on each station's streaming provider. Some stations may be temporarily unavailable.
